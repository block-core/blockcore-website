01

Angor

Decentralized Crowdfunding

Fund projects and get funded using Bitcoin.

Angor sets a new standard for decentralized crowdfunding. It combines the security of Bitcoin with the transparency of Nostr, so capital moves between founders and investors without a platform holding either side hostage.

Trustless crowdfunding
Funds are governed by time-locked Bitcoin contracts, not by an escrow account you have to trust.
Investor control
Unspent funds can be recovered at any time, keeping risk bounded and interests aligned with the founders.
Founder benefits
Streamlined fundraising in a trust-minimised environment, with no intermediary taking a cut or setting the rules.

Nostria

Your Social Network

Built for human connections. See your friends again.

Nostria is social without the noise. Built on the Nostr protocol, it gives you a feed shaped by the people you actually follow — and an identity no company can suspend, sell or algorithmically bury.

Start in two clicks
No email. No phone number. Your identity is generated locally and stays yours.
Own your data
Your social graph lives on relays you choose, not inside a walled garden you can be evicted from.
No ads, no tracking
Your attention is not for sale. Creators earn directly from their audience through Bitcoin zaps.

Blockchain stack

Legacy  /  Maintenance

Blockcore Blockchain

Blockcore developed a complete blockchain stack — full nodes, indexers, explorers and wallets — for teams launching their own chains. It remains open source and available, but it is no longer where the work is going.

Active development has moved to Angor and Nostria, which build directly on Bitcoin and Nostr rather than on a chain of our own.

Full nodes
Consensus, validation and networking for custom proof-of-stake and proof-of-work chains, written in C#.
Indexers
Address and transaction indexing exposed over a simple HTTP API, powering wallets and explorers.
Explorers
Chain-specific block explorers that individual networks can deploy without multi-chain overhead.
Wallets
Non-custodial HD browser wallet for coins, tokens, identities (DID) and NFTs across Blockcore, Stratis and Cirrus.

03

Principles

Four rules we do not trade away.

  • 01

    Open Source

    Every line is public. Fork it, audit it, or run it yourself.

  • 02

    Self-Sovereign

    Keys, identity and data stay with the user. We hold nothing on your behalf.

  • 03

    Censorship-Resistant

    There is no central switch to flip. Protocols route around interference.

  • 04

    Community-Driven

    Built in the open with contributors, not behind a private roadmap.
